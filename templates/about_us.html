About LocalLink

LocalLink is a digital marketplace that connects suppliers with vendors to reduce daily food waste and promote smarter sourcing. Our platform fosters an ecosystem where leftover stock becomes an opportunity for vendors to bid at affordable prices while suppliers recover value.

Our Mission

To minimize food waste, empower local suppliers, and help small businesses thrive by creating a transparent, real-time supply and demand platform.

Our Vision

To become the go-to hyperlocal food sourcing network, transforming how vendors and suppliers interact for a more sustainable tomorrow.
