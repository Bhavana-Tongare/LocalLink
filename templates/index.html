Welcome to LocalLink

Less Waste. More Profit. Smarter Sourcing

Reduce food waste and get raw materials at the best price through real-time bidding and discovery of local suppliers.

Solution

Trust & Credit System

Ratings, credit behavior, and transparent trust scores for all users.

  • Leaderboard of top-rated vendors and suppliers

  • Analytics for daily trends and performance tracking
