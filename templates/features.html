Key Features

Live Bidding System

Vendors can bid for leftover stock in real-time, creating a competitive, efficient, and fair marketplace.

Supplier & Vendor Dashboards

Dedicated dashboards for uploading stock, tracking bids, managing purchases, and analyzing trends.

Trust & Credit System

Build credibility through ratings, on-time deliveries, and payment behavior visible to other users.

Smart Notifications

Get alerts like “Bid Ending Soon”, “Stock Updated”, or “Bid Won!” to stay ahead and act fast.

Hyperlocal Discovery

Vendors can find suppliers near their location to reduce delivery time and cost.

Analytics & Insights

Suppliers and vendors can track their performance, bidding behavior, and market trends.
